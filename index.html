Eastern Melburnian — Story check

Two or more of these criteria, you’re onto something.

  1. 1Timeliness — it’s new, or newly known. Something happened, changed, or was just revealed.
  2. 2Impact — it affects people. The more people, or the bigger the effect on them, the stronger.
  3. 3Proximity — it’s close to your readers, by place or by connection.
  4. 4Prominence — it involves someone or something well-known.
  5. 5Conflict — there’s a disagreement, a fight, a tension, two sides.
  6. 6Novelty — it’s unusual, rare, surprising, or weird.
  7. 7Human interest — there’s a real person at the centre that readers can feel something about.
